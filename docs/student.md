# Student guide

During your quiz attempt, use **Like** or **Dislike** in a question's feedback
area. Select the active reaction again to remove it, or select the other reaction
to switch. Dislike explanations are optional, with a maximum of 2,000 characters.
After editing an explanation, activate **Save explanation**.

Wait for the saved confirmation; a failed save is not a saved reaction. Retry
when the connection recovers. If the question changed or your session expired,
follow the displayed reload/login instruction. Your quiz answers, timer and
submission use Moodle's normal controls. Feedback is separate from answering.

After submitting normally, use **Open consolidated question feedback** from the completed-attempt
entry area on the review or quiz activity page. It opens one page for that attempt.
Previously saved reactions are preselected, and you can add, edit or remove them
while you retain access to that completed attempt. The activity page's entry
points to your latest finished attempt; review an older attempt to use its link.

This page shows question numbers and permitted prompt text only. Some questions
show a number and feedback controls without prompt text because review is
restricted or the question cannot be safely represented. No “Prompt unavailable”
message is shown. Answer choices, your answers, correct answers and marks do not
appear here. Use Moodle's own review page for whatever review it permits.

You can use Tab to move between controls and Enter/Space to activate the focused
button. Reaction state and save/error messages are exposed to assistive tools.
Use the site's normal support channel if feedback remains unavailable; continue
your quiz using its native controls.

Feedback is not anonymous to authorized teaching staff. They may see your display
name beside a Dislike explanation within their permitted group/course. Do not
include sensitive or unrelated personal information. Staff notes/history are
private and are not shown to students. There are no automatic resolution notices.
